Independent Dispatcher Clarification (49 U.S.C. Compliance)

Tech Logistics LLC is an independent commercial truck dispatch service. Tech Logistics LLC is not a motor carrier, freight broker, or freight forwarder under Title 49 of the United States Code (49 U.S.C. § 13102).

1. Nature of Dispatch Services

Tech Logistics LLC acts exclusively as an authorized dispatch and administrative representative for motor carriers pursuant to written dispatch service agreements. Our role is strictly limited to back-office support, including load searching, freight rate negotiation with licensed property brokers, packet submission, route coordination, and invoicing support.

2. No Custody of Freight or Assumption of Liability

Tech Logistics LLC does not own commercial tractors or trailers, does not employ commercial drivers, does not take physical possession or custody of cargo, and does not issue bills of lading. All freight transportation is conducted by the licensed, insured commercial motor carriers that contract our dispatch services. Tech Logistics LLC assumes zero legal liability for cargo loss, damage, spoilage, theft, transit delays, highway accidents, or vehicular damage.

3. Independent Contractor Relationship

Motor carriers represented by Tech Logistics LLC retain complete operating discretion over their business, including final authority to accept or reject any load negotiated by our dispatchers, selection of travel routes, compliance with Hours of Service (HOS) rules, and adherence to all United States Department of Transportation (USDOT) and Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) regulations.

4. Financial & Billing Clarity

Tech Logistics LLC does not collect freight transportation monies from shippers or brokers on behalf of carriers, nor do we issue payments for cargo transport. Freight compensation is paid directly by brokers or shippers to the motor carrier (or the carrier's designated factoring institution). Tech Logistics LLC invoices carriers separately for administrative dispatch services rendered, appearing on bank statements under the descriptor Extreme Dispatch Fee, processed strictly in arrears via Stripe.
