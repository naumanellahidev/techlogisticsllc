About Tech Logistics LLC

Professional back-office truck dispatch services dedicated to independent motor carriers and owner-operators across the United States.

Who We Are

Carrier Advocacy & Dispatch Leadership

Tech Logistics LLC is a professional truck dispatch service company organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming. We provide dedicated back-office dispatch, load sourcing, rate negotiation, and administrative documentation services to commercial motor carriers and owner-operators throughout the United States.

In the modern freight ecosystem, truck drivers and small fleet owners face significant administrative burdens. Sourcing quality loads, conducting credit checks on freight brokers, negotiating detention and layover rates, and processing carrier packets require constant telephone contact and screen time while drivers need to keep their focus entirely on road safety. Tech Logistics LLC steps in as an authorized, independent dispatch representative, managing the heavy administrative lifting so our carrier partners can maximize revenue and operate efficiently.

We operate on a straightforward, performance-driven business model: we do not charge any upfront fees or retainers. We earn our service fee only after a load is successfully sourced, booked, transported, and completed.

Regulatory Standards

Legal Compliance & Tax Obligations

Tech Logistics LLC conducts its business in strict accordance with applicable United States federal and state laws. We maintain full registration and good standing within the State of Wyoming, timely satisfy all municipal, state, and federal tax obligations, and maintain clear separation between motor carrier operations, freight brokerage, and independent dispatch services.

Statutory Independent Dispatcher Clarification

Tech Logistics LLC acts strictly as an independent dispatch service on behalf of motor carriers pursuant to signed dispatch service agreements. Tech Logistics LLC is not a motor carrier, freight broker, or freight forwarder as defined under Title 49 of the United States Code. We do not own transportation equipment, take physical custody or possession of cargo, issue bills of lading under our own name, or assume cargo liability. All transportation services are provided exclusively by the authorized motor carriers we represent.

Operating Values

Our Operating Principles

Carrier-Centric Representation

We represent the carrier, not the broker. Our fiduciary duty is to obtain the best possible rate per mile, fair detention terms, and favorable delivery windows for the driver.

Post-Service Billing Integrity

By collecting fees only after dispatch deliverables are completed, we align our financial success directly with the success and satisfaction of our carrier clients.
