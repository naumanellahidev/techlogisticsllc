How the Engagement Works

A transparent, sequential workflow from initial carrier onboarding to post-service invoicing.

Strict Policy: No Upfront Charges

Tech Logistics LLC does not charge any upfront deposits, setup fees, or retainers. We bill only after the dispatch service for your agreed loads has been fully completed and delivered.

  1. 1

    Onboarding and Paperwork

    We execute an independent dispatch service agreement, gather your carrier profile (MC/DOT authority, Certificate of Insurance, W-9, and equipment specifications), and establish your target freight preferences, home-time schedules, and rate requirements.

  2. 2

    Load Sourcing and Rate Negotiation

    Our dispatchers monitor nationwide freight markets and negotiate directly with verified freight brokers to secure loads at the highest available market rates, verifying lane dynamics, fuel surcharges, and detention terms before presentation.

  3. 3

    Booking and Scheduling

    Upon your verbal or written confirmation, we execute the broker rate confirmation, submit required carrier packets, secure pickup and delivery appointments, and transmit complete dispatch sheets with clear driving instructions.

  4. 4

    Service Completed

    The assigned load is picked up, transported, and safely delivered to the consignee. You submit the signed Bill of Lading (BOL) or Proof of Delivery (POD), and we assist in assembling all necessary documentation for your records or factoring submission.

  5. 5

    Invoice Issued and Fee Collected

    Only after your dispatch service is completely fulfilled, Tech Logistics LLC issues an itemized service invoice. Charges appear on your card and bank statement as Extreme Dispatch Fee, processed securely via Stripe in accordance with your signed service agreement.
