3. Description of the Dispatch Services Provided

Tech Logistics LLC provides independent commercial dispatch services for authorized motor carriers. Specific scope includes:

  • Freight identification and sourcing via authorized load board databases and direct broker channels;
  • Rate negotiation with licensed freight brokers to establish commercial freight compensation;
  • Submission of carrier onboarding packets, insurance certificates, and W-9 forms to brokers;
  • Coordination of pickup and delivery appointment schedules and routing logistics;
  • Administrative document collection including rate confirmations, Bills of Lading (BOL), and Proof of Delivery (POD) receipts;
  • Operational back-office support including assistance with detention and accessorial claims.

4. Client Obligations

To receive Dispatch Services, Client warrants and agrees to maintain at all times:

  • Active Operating Authority: Valid, active, and unrevoked motor carrier operating authority (MC number and USDOT registration) issued by the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA);
  • Insurance Coverage: Commercial motor vehicle insurance in full force and effect, with minimum limits of $1,000,000 Auto Liability and $100,000 Cargo Liability (or higher as required by specific shippers/brokers);
  • Accurate Documentation: Client must provide true, complete, and unexpired copies of authority certificates, W-9, Certificate of Insurance naming brokers as certificate holders, and Notice of Assignment (if factoring);
  • Timely Communication: Immediate notification to Tech Logistics LLC regarding any equipment breakdowns, transit delays, detention incidents, or shipper/receiver discrepancies;
  • Equipment Safety & Compliance: Ensuring commercial motor vehicles, drivers, and safety equipment comply with all federal, state, and local safety regulations, including 49 C.F.R. regulations and Hours of Service (HOS) mandates.

5. Fees, Invoicing, and Payment Terms (Extreme Dispatch Fee)

Strict Post-Service Billing: Tech Logistics LLC charges no upfront deposits, enrollment fees, or retainers. All dispatch service fees are invoiced and collected strictly after the dispatch services for a given load or billing cycle have been fully completed and delivered.

Calculation of Fees: Fees are calculated based on the signed individual Service Agreement, typically structured as an agreed percentage (e.g., 40%) of the gross revenue of loads dispatched, or an agreed flat weekly rate (e.g., 30%).
